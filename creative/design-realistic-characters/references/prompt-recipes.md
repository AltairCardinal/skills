# Prompt recipes

Use these as structures, not as fixed faces. Replace every bracketed field and remove unused lines.

## Identity discovery

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: neutral adult character identity test; not a finished movie still
Primary request: Create one specific-looking [cultural context] [adult gender], around [age], who feels [ordinary social impression]. Do not make the person a model, hero, villain, victim, or demographic average.

Facial identity fingerprint — subtle and anatomically plausible:
- face silhouette: [shape], with the subject’s [side] contour [small difference];
- eyes: [spacing and size]; the subject’s [side] eyelid [difference];
- brows: [density and shape], with [directional difference];
- nose: [bridge relationship], [tip], and [nostril relationship];
- mouth and chin: [lip relationship], [mouth asymmetry], and [chin];
- localized marks: [one or two marks in exact regions];
- hair, ears, or eyewear: [stable irregularity].

Expression: emotionally neutral, mouth closed, relaxed brows, attentive gaze, no acting.
Scene: plain real wall with no narrative props.
Photography: unretouched natural-light identity photograph, normal perspective, moderate depth of field, neutral color.
Constraints: one adult person; preserve mild left/right relationships; do not symmetrize, beautify, harmonize, idealize, or exaggerate.
Avoid: [casting risks], beauty portrait, influencer face, waxy skin, uniform pores, HDR, glamour light, film grain, artificial blur, dramatic grading, text, border, watermark.
```

## Sympathetic without idealization

Translate “sympathetic” into relaxed social access:

- use a soft but adult facial contour;
- keep eyes ordinarily sized and visually available, not enlarged;
- keep brows relaxed rather than worried;
- use a receptive gaze and closed relaxed mouth;
- add slightly awkward everyday hair, ears, glasses, or clothing;
- forbid advertisement smiles, infantilization, saintliness, and glamour.

Do not claim these anatomical choices prove goodness. Story behavior must earn audience attachment.

## Gentle without helplessness

Translate “gentle” into calm openness, conflict avoidance, or a listening habit. Explicitly forbid pleading, coyness, submissive posture, permanent sadness, doll eyes, childlike proportions, and victim performance. Preserve adult agency in the neutral identity pass.

## Photography pass

```text
Input image: approved identity anchor; preserve face, body proportions, localized marks, hairline, and stable eyewear.
Change only: environment, wardrobe, framing, lens behavior, exposure, and color response.
Scene: [specific place].
Photography: [camera viewpoint and medium].
Lighting: [practical sources and exposure].
Expression: neutral; performance will be added later.
Avoid: facial redesign, beauty correction, symmetry increase, skin replacement, new moles or scars.
```

## Performance pass

```text
Input image: approved identity and photography reference.
Micro-situation: [one concrete event the character perceives].
Visible response: [one gaze behavior] and [one muscle or posture behavior].
Keep: identity, environment, wardrobe, camera, and lighting unchanged.
Avoid: emotion keyword stacks, symbolic hands, open-mouth shorthand, crying brows, added sweat or tears unless physically required.
```

## Continuity pass

Attach the approved identity anchor and the most recent approved scene frame when the tool supports both. Repeat stable anatomy and localized marks. Describe only the new action and camera change. If identity and scene controls compete, preserve identity first and simplify the shot.
